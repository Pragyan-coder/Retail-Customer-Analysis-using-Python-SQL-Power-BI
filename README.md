### ✅ Problem statement:

A leading retail company wants to understand its customers’ shopping behavior to improve sales, customer satisfaction, and long-term loyalty. The management team has noticed changes in purchasing patterns across demographics, product categories, and sales channels (online vs. offline). They are particularly interested in uncovering which factors, such as **discounts, reviews, seasons, or payment preferences,** drive consumer decisions and repeat purchases.

⚡As an Analyst, I am tasked with analyzing the company’s consumer behavior dataset to answer the overarching business questions

### ✒️The Takeaway:

Through this project, I learned how to execute **Data Analytics** workflow, moving from raw data to actionable business insights. Gained experience in business problem-solving, specifically in analyzing customer demographics, shopping trends, and the efficacy of loyalty programs.

1.**Revenue Source Diversification:** Non-subscribers generate 73% of total revenue, showing the business relies more on transactional sales than recurring subscriptions.

2.**Demographic Spend Drivers:** Young Adults are the most valuable segment, leading in both total purchase volume and average customer ratings.

3.**Discount Paradox:** Discounted transactions yielded 14% lower average values than non-discounted ones, highlighting the need to protect profit margins.

4.**Customer Segmentation:** SQL logic to classify "New," "Returning," and "Loyal" buyers enables targeted retention strategies based on purchase history.

5.**Operational Optimization:** Identifying preferences like PayPal and specific shipping types by demographic allows for more efficient logistics and checkout experiences
